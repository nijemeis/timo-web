TIMO

Timo Time Tracker

Presence-based time registration. One proximity UUID for every beacon; major identifies the company, minor the location. Entering a beacon region checks you in, leaving checks you out — with a sound, a notification and an on-screen confirmation.

Try it
  1. Send a sign-in link, then use demo: open the link on the inbox screen.
  2. Grant the three permissions — each opens the platform's own system dialog.
  3. In the simulator under the phone, Walk in at a beacon: you hear a chime and see the confirmation. Leave checks you out.
  4. Switch to Phone locked and walk in again — the confirmation arrives as a lock-screen notification.
  5. Walking in at another beacon while checked in closes the first registration and opens a new one at the new location.
  6. Use +1h to move the clock, then open Registrations to filter by period, location or “Needs attention”, and request a correction.
Platform differences shown
Dynamic Island vs. punch-hole status bar · large titles & chevron back vs. Material top app bar · tab bar vs. navigation bar with pill indicator · iOS vs. Material switches, alerts and lock-screen notifications · “Allow all the time” location wording on Android.
02

Check-in / check-out confirmation — options

Shown when the app is open. 1b is the chosen direction and is used in the prototype.
1aFull-screen takeover
Unmissable at the door; big time for a glance. Best for shared-entrance moments. Auto-closes after 6 s.
1bBottom sheetChosen
Keeps context, shows time + worked hours + location, and offers a correction right away if it's wrong.
1cTop banner
Lightest touch; mirrors the system notification. Fine once users trust the tracking.
03

Home — options

1dStatus + running timer
Answers “am I checked in?” first, then today and the week against contract hours.
1eDay timeline + week
Hours-first. The day track shows gaps and location switches; Monday's unclosed registration reads as “?”.
